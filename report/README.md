Each point is an entry point, labelled. The x-axis is hardware cycles at `native` (what rule 1 reads); the y-axis is the trade-off named in the title. Lower and further left is better on both. **Blue**, joined by a step line: the frontier of the two axes shown, among every measured entry: no other entry is better on one without being worse on the other, by more than ε = 5 % or, on cycles, the A/A band, and a cycles advantage callgrind's count contradicts does not count. **Orange ring**: on the frontier the verdict reads at this workload, which weighs all six axes (the four decode-side ones for a decode-only entry): at a Kynos workload rule 1's, where an entry is ringed if none of the eligible entries that have not failed conformance dominates it; at any other, the *deserves to exist* frontier, where none of the entries that have not failed conformance does.
